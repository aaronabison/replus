FAQ

Before you tap install.

Is Replus free?

Yes. Replus is free to download and free to create an account on iPhone, Android, and PC.

How is Replus a social ecosystem app?

Replus is built with a combination of new features and platforms that no other social networking app has given a try. It's not just a social network, it's a social ecosystem.

Why is Replus subscription-based?

We've packed features and platforms and we do not show ads or sell/share user data to anyone including partners.

Why does it ask for camera and microphone access?

Those permissions power in-app photo, video, and voice features. You can skip them during setup and turn them on later from your device settings whenever you're ready.

Can I use one account on more than one device?

Yes — sign in with the same account on your phone and PC to pick up right where you left off.

Stuck during setup?

Reach out anytime from the Contact page and we'll help you get connected.
