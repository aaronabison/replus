Aaron Abison

Founder & CEO, Replus Stacks

"I never gave up my plan to build and launch a very important app that has become what millions of people want to have. "

— Aaron Abison

Aaron Abison is a tech entrepreneur, computer programmer and the founder and CEO of Replus Stacks LTD which is the parent company of Replus social networking platform.

He built Replus with the intention of reforming the Internet and the social media industry after meaningful observations and findings that the social networking industry is lacking the right experience billions of users want to see at the same time.
