Founder's note

Aaron Abison

Founder & CEO, Replus Stacks

"Our mission is simple: make every scroll mean something."

— Aaron Abison

Aaron Abison is a tech entrepreneur, computer programmer, and the founder and CEO of Replus Stacks LTD, the parent company of the Replus social networking platform.

He built Replus after noticing something missing from social media: you could scroll for hours and never realize the impact and how many people around the world shared your exact taste. Replus closes that gap, combining all social elements into one ecosystem built around real connection.

Why we exist

Not less scrolling. More meaning in it.

+

Feeds, plus meaning

Your feed doesn't go away — it just goes further. See who else, anywhere in the world, shares what you're into, from the podcasts you love to the tracks you can't stop playing.

+

One ecosystem, every connection

Let's Read, Replus Podcasts, and Replus Circles live inside one app — so what you discover in one place shows up in the connections you make everywhere else.

+

No ads, no data selling

Replus runs on convenient subscriptions, not your attention or your data — so the incentives stay aligned with you.

Right now

What I'm focused on.

+

I'm focused on building technology that actually matters — tools people use because they add something to their day, not because they can't put them down.

+

I started Replus to reform social networking — not by getting people to scroll less, but by making every scroll worth something. That's the whole idea behind connecting Replus to the hobbies and interests you already care about.

+

Events and Calendly-style scheduling are already live inside Replus, and I'm working on what comes next.
