Founder's note

Aaron Abison

Founder & CEO, Replus Stacks

"Our mission is simple: build the app that adds to your life instead of stealing from it."

— Aaron Abison

Aaron Abison is a tech entrepreneur, computer programmer, and the founder and CEO of Replus Stacks LTD, the parent company of the Replus social networking platform.

He built Replus after seeing a clear gap in social networking: people were spending more time scrolling than connecting. Replus closes that gap by combining reading, podcasts, communities, and messaging into one ecosystem built around real connection — not endless engagement.

Why we exist

The problem with social media today.

+

Built to hold attention

Most platforms are optimized to keep you scrolling, not to leave you better off. Replus is built around the opposite goal.

+

One ecosystem, not another feed

Let's Read, Replus Podcasts, and Replus Circles live inside one app — useful on their own, better together.

+

No ads, no data selling

Replus runs on subscriptions, not your attention or your data — so the incentives stay aligned with you.
